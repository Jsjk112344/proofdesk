Marigold Lane Bakery

We bake sourdough loaves, croissants and seasonal fruit tarts before dawn every day.

Our small team of six bakers trained in traditional methods, and we mill part of our flour on site.

Celebration cakes can be ordered in the shop two weeks ahead. We do not take online orders.

Visit us on the corner of Marigold Lane; opening hours are posted on the door.

We source butter and eggs from farms within forty miles and change the menu with the seasons.
