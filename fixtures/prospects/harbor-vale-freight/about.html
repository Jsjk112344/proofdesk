About Harbor & Vale

We started as a two-van courier in 2009 and now operate 24,000 square metres of warehousing across two sites.

Our operations team coordinates inbound deliveries from more than 300 suppliers, and our back office processes the paperwork that comes with them.

We believe in checking the small things: a delivery note that does not match its purchase order is caught at the door, not at month end.

How we work

  • Goods-in staff match supplier delivery notes to purchase orders manually before put-away.
  • Accounts payable keys supplier invoices into the finance system and reconciles them against signed proof of delivery.
  • Customer statements are produced weekly from the warehouse management system.
