# Demo walkthrough

Open [SalesDesk](https://salesdesk.149.28.51.139.sslip.io) in a fresh browser session.
The Vultr workers prepare a benchmark example with a ready packet and an example prospect
with an unsent draft. Seeded approval is labelled; your later changes need a fresh approval.

1. **Home:** two entry doors and the dashboard. Choose **Analyse a campaign**.
2. **Result:** inspect the historical purchase-rate difference and uncertainty. Analyst
   activity shows actual saved steps; expand Verification on a step for details. Run again
   to see the worker progress. Replay is labelled when an accepted response is reused.
3. **Next move:** edit the proposed move or add a note and save. Unsaved edits cannot be
   approved. Enter a preparation time or leave it empty, then **Approve & schedule**.
4. **Walk away:** close the tab while the worker prepares. Return to **Ready for review**,
   the packet summary and Markdown download. Missing inputs are retained in the packet.
5. **Verification:** source, saved plan, reconciliation, Docker evidence, model receipts
   and job history are available behind disclosures.
6. **Starting from zero:** inspect the seeded Harbor Vale example brief, demonstration
   decision and unsent outreach draft. Pages and demonstration inputs are labelled synthetic.
   Mark the draft reviewed, then edit/save to see that review no longer covers the new version.
7. **New work:** New analysis plan and New prospect remain available within session quotas.

The benchmark is the genuine historical campaign, re-analysed retrospectively. SalesDesk
has not launched a new campaign or obtained new sales. See [demo-script.md](demo-script.md)
for the recording script and [submission.md](submission.md) for prepared form fields.
