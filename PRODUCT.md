# SalesDesk product

## Register

product

## Users

Sales managers evaluating a campaign and technical sellers researching first customers.
Hackathon judges need a working, seeded journey with a clear worker trace.

## Product Purpose

Two doors share one loop: measure or research, propose a next move, approve, and return
to work prepared on Vultr. The home screen makes the two choices clear.

## Brand Personality

Direct, practical, evidence-led. Preserve the existing forest-green workspace identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid prominent infrastructure explanations, dense hash lists and duplicate approval controls.
The owner requests Verification disclosures and fewer tabs.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the commercial result and the next useful action.
- Keep the current visual vocabulary; remove clutter before adding controls.
- Show actual worker progress and honest interpretation provenance.
- Keep every visible control usable in public-demo sessions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard focus, labelled inputs, semantic disclosures, responsive layout and
reduced-motion support. Never use color alone to convey job status.
